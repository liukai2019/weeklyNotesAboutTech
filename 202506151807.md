## ✍️ 改进建议（逐段讲解 + 示例修改）

---

### ✅ 第一段（引言）

**原句：**

> The graph illustrates the percentage of the population in four Asian countries living in cities from 1970 to 2020, it also contains the predictions for 2030 and 2040.

**问题：**

- 两个句子之间缺少连词，用逗号连接是**逗号拼接错误（comma splice）**
    
- "The population in four countries living in cities" 结构不太清晰
    

**修改：**

> The line graph shows the proportion of people living in cities in four Asian countries—Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand, and Indonesia—from 1970 to 2020, with projections for 2030 and 2040.

---

### ✅ 第二段（总览）

**原句：**

> The population of four contries living in cities all increased since 1970, though the different growth rate. The Malaysia's percentage of total population remained highest over the 20th-year period with the prediction of still ranking first among the four contries.

**问题：**

- 拼写错误："contries" → "countries"
    
- 语法错误："The Malaysia's" 不对，国家名不能加冠词
    
- “20th-year period” 表达不清，意思模糊
    
- 表达不自然：“with the prediction of still ranking…”
    

**修改建议：**

> Overall, the proportion of urban population increased in all four countries, although at different rates. Malaysia consistently had the highest percentage throughout the period and is projected to remain in the lead until 2040.

---

### ✅ 第三段（细节 1）

**原句：**

> In 1970, the percentage of Philippines was slightly high than the Malaysia's, they were both about 30 percentage of population living in cities, but the Malaysia's percentage stayed first rank since 1970 only except a very short time in 1990. In 2020, the number of Malaysia living in cities in approximately 75 percentage, which is also the highest number in all four contries.

**问题：**

- 表达混乱（“the percentage of Philippines”，应为“the percentage in the Philippines”）
    
- “high” 应为 “higher”，比较级错误
    
- 多处语法和逻辑问题（“the number of Malaysia living in cities...”）
    

**修改建议：**

> In 1970, the percentage of city dwellers in the Philippines was slightly higher than in Malaysia, with both countries around 30%. However, Malaysia quickly overtook the Philippines and remained in the top position, except for a brief period around 1990. By 2020, Malaysia’s urban population had risen to approximately 75%, the highest among the four nations.

---

### ✅ 第四段（细节 2）

**原句：**

> The ramaining three contries included Philippines, Thailand, Indonesia, their percentage in 2020 is relatively low if compared with Malaysia, the second rank's percentage in 2020 is about 50, which is Indonesia's percentage, its percentage is lowest at 1970.

**问题：**

- 拼写错误：“ramaining” → “remaining”
    
- 冠词、逗号使用错误，句子结构混乱
    
- 缺少清晰地描述 Thailand 的趋势
    

**修改建议：**

> The other three countries—Indonesia, the Philippines, and Thailand—had lower urban populations compared to Malaysia. Indonesia showed the most significant growth, increasing from just over 10% in 1970 to about 50% in 2020, overtaking both Thailand and the Philippines. Thailand's growth was more gradual, rising from under 20% in 1970 to just over 40% in 2020.
