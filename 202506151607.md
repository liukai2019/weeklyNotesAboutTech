### 改进后的范文（约 180 字）：

The graph illustrates the number of participants in five different activities at a social centre in Melbourne, Australia from 2000 to 2020.

Overall, the film club remained the most popular activity throughout the period, while table tennis saw the most significant growth. In contrast, amateur dramatics experienced a steady decline.

The film club consistently attracted around 60 to 65 participants over the 20 years. Table tennis, starting at about 20 participants in 2000, increased sharply to reach over 50 by 2020. Amateur dramatics peaked in 2005 but then declined steadily, falling to the lowest level by 2020.

Musical performances had no participants before 2005 but showed steady growth afterward, although their numbers remained relatively modest. Martial arts maintained a relatively stable number of participants, with only slight fluctuations.

In conclusion, film club and table tennis were the most popular activities, while amateur dramatics and musical performances were the least favored by 2020.
