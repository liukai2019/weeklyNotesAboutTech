Design the smallest believable demo for dns-watch v0.

Please define:
1. what test process to use,
2. what DNS action that process should perform,
3. what output dns-watch should show,
4. what would count as a passing demo,
5. what could make the demo misleading,
6. how to keep the demo simple and repeatable.

Important rules:
- Favor a minimal, reproducible demo.
- Do not require a complex lab environment.
- Do not assume polished tooling.
- Prefer a demo that proves one thing clearly.

Use this output structure:

## Demo goal
## Test process
## DNS action
## Expected dns-watch output
## Passing condition
## What could mislead us
## Simplest repeatable demo
