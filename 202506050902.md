This graph contains the statistics trend of the participants number of 5 activities in at one social centre. Firstly, the film club’s participants number stays almost unchanged during 20 years and it’s also the biggest part in all activities. Secondly, table tennis is more and more popular, you can see the table tennis line goes up every year, maybe it could reach the number of film club next year, the goes up rate is relatively high. On the another hand, The number of Amateur dramatics goes down since 2005 every year, it’s at the lowest level in 2020. There are 2 activities left, their data is not straightforward as mentioned above, basically they are at the medium level of all activities, one of the two activities, Musical Performance is zero before 2005, but since that, it goes up every year, though it’s still just higher than Amateur dramatics in 2020, it remains going up. The last activity, Martial arts, it’s stays at its level since 2000.

The main feature you could in 2000 is that Film club is still the most popular activity, the Amateur dramatics is the least popular activity,
